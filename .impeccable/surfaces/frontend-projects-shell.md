THESIS: The shell of a contemporary dental clinic: warm, calm and unmistakably clinical, organized around orientation rather than a metaphor. It refuses the artifact costume (label sheet, map, card, ledger) and the generic SaaS dashboard alike.

OWN-WORLD: Warm porcelain ground (#f6f4ef), ink (#1c2422), muted sage-grey (#6e7772), pale oak (#a9825f), clinic primary and accent as working colours. Onest Variable for the shell's voice, tabular numerals for data. Borders and rules instead of shadows; 8px controls, 14px panels; one primary action per region. Signature: the dayline under the header — current date and time, active section and its group — a quiet reception-desk line that tells you where you are before anything else.

STORY: The receptionist opens the shell and knows the day, the section and the next action without reading a manual. The dentist moves between patient and agenda without losing context. The administrator sees the clinic's own name and colour, not the product's.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px header: clinic lockup left, dayline (date · section · group) centre, actions right (CaroIA, activity, language, user). 248px sidebar on a warm ground with grouped rows, a solid primary marker on the active item, and a collapse control. Main area on warm porcelain with the remote page's own heading and one primary action. No footer. On mobile the sidebar becomes a drawer and the dayline condenses.
